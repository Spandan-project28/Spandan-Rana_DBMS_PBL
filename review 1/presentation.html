2. Problem Identification & Scope

Resolving data anomalies and multi-hop seat clashes through relational normalization

Core Challenges & Solutions
Problem 1: Data Anomalies & Redundancy Schema Architecture
The Flaw: Monolithic Unnormalized Data

Naive systems combine train schedules, stations, and passenger bookings into denormalized tables, triggering dangerous data corruption:

Insertion Anomaly Update Inconsistency Deletion Data Loss
Our Relational Solution: 3NF Entity Decomposition

We decomposed the schema into 10 individual atomic entity tables (Train, Station, Coach, Seat, Passenger, etc.). Every non-key attribute depends strictly on the Primary Key with zero transitive dependencies.

10 Atomic Tables Zero Transitive Dependency 100% 3NF Integrity
Problem 2: Monolithic Seat Overlap Concurrency Model
The Flaw: End-to-End Route Seat Locking

Locking an entire seat for the full train run blocks valid intermediate passengers (huge revenue loss) or causes double-booking seat clashes when multiple legs are booked.

Clashing Whole-Route Lock (Delhi → Varanasi) [BLOCKED]
Our Relational Solution: Segment-Wise Granular Allocation

Introduced the associative JOURNEY_SEGMENT entity. A seat is partitioned into atomic station-to-station legs, enabling multiple passengers to share the same physical seat across disjoint legs with zero conflicts.

Delhi (1)Agra (2)Kanpur (3)Varanasi (4)
User A (1→2)
User B (2→4) [NO CONFLICT]
